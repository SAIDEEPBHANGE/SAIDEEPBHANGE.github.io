### Geo-Location Based Emergency Ambulance Booking Service
International Journal (IJARSCT) · April 2023 · Volume 3, Issue 7 · Peer reviewed

This research presents a GIS-integrated Android framework designed to act as a digital intermediary between patients and providers, specifically targeting the Golden Hour in emergency medical services. By leveraging Google Maps API and cloud-based real-time data synchronization, the system enables location tracking, proximity-based driver allocation, and intelligent route navigation. The proposed three-tier architecture demonstrates significant improvements in booking latency, navigation accuracy, and emergency response efficiency, effectively optimizing medical logistics and reducing human error in time-critical scenarios.
